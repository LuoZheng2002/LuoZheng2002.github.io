# Project Experiences
## "Crafty Piggies 3D" Game Development
(Include youtube video for the trailer: https://youtu.be/zd16g53LavQ with caption "Game Trailer")
(Include youtube video for the demo day trailer reaction: https://youtu.be/FIf5dbHN1ss with caption "Demo Day Trailer Reaction")

[Play the game on Windows and Mac](https://luozheng2002.itch.io/crafty-piggies-gold)

"Crafty Piggies 3D" is a physics-based 3D game that allows players to build customized vehicles through a grid-based system and control their vehicles to solve puzzles and reach destinations. It is inspired by Bad Piggies and Genshin Impact, where the former features a 2D grid-based building system and level-based progression, and the latter features an open world and a dedicated storyline. We tried to fuse the two genres together to create a game that is both mechanically fun and appealing in storytelling.

This is a capstone project for graduating from the University of Michigan, where I worked with Yikai Li, Gabriel Froehner and Javier Guerrero, instructed by Prof. Austin Yarger.

## Table Tennis Feeding Machine
(Include youtube videos: https://youtu.be/Eg9H1ohUx7M https://youtu.be/D8YpBVKRTdU)

This is an Embedded Systems course project, where each team decides their own topic. 
Our team is composed of Zheng Luo (me), Zhiheng Alex Zhang, Xinting Jiang and Mingjian Li, and I was the team leader.

Our project features a vehicle with omniwheels topped with a pinch roller that feeds table tennis balls. It can move and rotate freely in all directions, adjust launching angle, launching speed and even spin.

One interesting fact about this project is that the pinch roller (the top white part of the vehicle) is fully handmade by us, with a 3D printed barrel, embedded with two brushless motors that spin independently and accumulate inertia for a full-speed launching. Stepper motors are used for pitch angle and launch triggering. It was a quite crazy idea from me, given that we have to make every piece to function correctly, or otherwise the whole project will fail.

## AI Short Play Making
(Include link https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ATH4GIfw3lE)

An AI short play depicting a possible parallel world where humans were fooled by aliens into believing they have invented LLM, but it turns out that the intelligent words from LLMs are synthesized by the aliens and presented to the human eyes. This is to banter the fact that we have absolutely no idea whether a deep learning method will work until we see the experiment results, and when a method turns out to be working, we go back to justify it with all kinds of theories, while if the outcome were bad, we would throw the same method into trashcan and use theories to justify why it doesn't work.

## Personal Music Box Project

(Include link https://youtu.be/2swCZ2V19NA with caption "Appearance and Internals Display")
(Include link https://youtu.be/g1gPUi-Pj-o with caption "Functionality Demo")

This is a personal project whose initial purpose was to be given to a girl I secretly liked, but the outcome beyond the project itself does not end well.

It is a music box that can play music, emit beautiful lights, has a spinning part, and plays a weird sound effect after the power is fully turned off (yes, this is a feature I'm really proud of). The initial design involves planet gears that speed down the brushless motor and enable two platform rings to spin in opposite directions. However, I eventually found that the friction and noise caused by 3D printed and laser-cut gears were too much, so I had to give up the fancy plan and connect the crystal piano to the brushless motor directly.

I got familiar with various skills through this project, including PCB milling, 3D printing and laser cutting.
